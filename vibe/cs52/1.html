Build a Paper Robot

← Back

A fun warm-up craft before we code!

Computers and robots are everywhere — let's make our own paper friend first!

  • Grab colored paper, scissors, glue, markers, and googly eyes (if you have them).
  • Fold and cut simple shapes: rectangle body, square head, circle buttons.
  • Draw arms, legs, antennas — make it look like a friendly robot!
  • Give your robot a name and write what job it does (e.g., "Bleep helps sort toys").

Now imagine telling your robot exactly what to do, step by step — that's what programming is like!
