What is a variable?

A variable is like a labeled box that holds something that can change — a number, a word, or even a color!

Example: Your robot has a "name" box. Right now it says "Bleep", but you can change it to "Zog", "Mr. Robot" or whatever you like later.

Play with cards!

  • Place a robot right below lower left square of a chessboard.
  • Take the Joker card and call it your "steps variable" and place right below the robot.
  • Take a number 6 card and place it on the Joker card — that's the value and the number of steps to move the robot in the direction it's facing.
  • Let's reset and place the robot back at its starting position.
  • Now change the 6 to 7 — see? The box stays the same, but the value inside changed! Move the robot 7 steps forward.

In real coding, we say things like: steps = 0 then later steps = steps + 10.

Quick challenge

Change the steps variables on your cards to make your robot move forward. Reset the robot back to its starting position each time.

Things to think about

What happens if you use a number greater than 8? What happens if the card is a King or Queen? What do you do?
