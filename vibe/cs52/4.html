Do it again… and again!

A loop tells the computer to repeat something many times — like singing the same chorus in a song.

Card loop game

  • Pick 5 cards and line them up.
  • Make a "loop card" (write "REPEAT 3 TIMES" on an extra card or note).
  • Instruction: "For each card in the line, wave your hand once — but do the whole line 3 times because of the loop!"
  • Count how many waves you did (15!).

Without the loop you'd only wave 5 times. Loops save time!

Try this

Make a loop that says "jump 4 times" — then put it around drawing 3 stars. How many jumps total?
