Mission 7 – Loops: Repeat the Magic

Big Idea: Sometimes the best paths have repeating patterns — like going forward, turn, forward, turn, forward... over and over. Instead of writing the same cards again and again, we use loop cards to say "do this part multiple times"!

A loop card (with 1, 2, or 3 on it) tells the robot: "Repeat the next group of actions this many times." It's like telling a friend "jump three times" instead of saying "jump, jump, jump".

New Loop Cards

  • Loop 1 — do the next block once (useful for testing)
  • Loop 2 — repeat the next block twice
  • Loop 3 — repeat the next block three times

How it works: Place a loop card, then place the actions you want to repeat right after it (forwards, turns, etc.). The robot does those actions the number of times shown on the loop card.

In real coding this looks like:
for i in range(3):
    forward(1)
    turn_right()

Quick Playground Challenge

  • Place robot at start, facing up.
  • Try: Loop 3 1 R
  • What happens? → The robot repeats "forward 1, turn right" three times → makes a square spiral!
  • Now change to Loop 2 2 L → repeats "forward 2, turn left" twice → bigger turns!
  • Experiment: What shape do you get with Loop 3 1 R Loop 2 1 L?
