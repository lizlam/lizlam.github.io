Decisions, decisions!

Computers ask questions all the time: "Is it raining?" → if yes then take umbrella, else wear sunglasses.

That's an if-then-else statement!

Card game: Red or Black?

  • Spread out 10–15 playing cards face down.
  • Pick one secretly and look at it.
  • Tell your friend: "If the card is red, then jump once. Else (black), clap twice."
  • Flip the card — did your friend do the right action?

Try making your own rules: "If number > 7 then shout 'High!', else whisper 'Low'."

Mission 6 – Mystery Card Challenge

Big Idea: The goal itself holds a secret! Use if-then-else to decide if reaching the star is a win or "try again" based on what you reveal when you flip it.

Your Quest: Program the robot to navigate across the 8×8 grid to the mystery star square (marked 🂠 — a face-down playing card). When the robot lands there, flip the card and apply your rule:

If the card is RED (or HIGH / EVEN — choose your rule!), THEN WIN! 🎉 (cheer, high-five, success!)
Else → LOSE! 😅 (reset robot, reshuffle card, try again)

Play multiple rounds — the card is random each time (a friend can secretly place or swap it), so the same path + conditional might win or lose. That's the power of decisions with uncertainty!
