A zine is a tiny homemade magazine — let's make one about your paper robot!

How to fold a mini zine

  1. Take one sheet of paper.
  2. Fold it in half, then in half again, then in half one more time (like a little book).
  3. Cut a slit in the middle fold, then push the sides to make a little booklet with 8 pages.
  4. Draw your robot on the cover.
  5. Inside pages: its name (variable), what it does if/when (conditionals), how it repeats actions (loops).

Decorate with markers, stickers, or tiny code snippets like "score = score + 1"!
